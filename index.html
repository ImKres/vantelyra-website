How healthy is your company’s IT?

Eight yes-or-no questions about the basics most small businesses miss. It takes about two minutes.

Your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to send them to us.

  1. Is your business data backed up automatically, and have you tested restoring it? Backups that have never been restored often fail when you need them most.
  2. Does everyone use two-step sign-in (MFA) for email and key apps? Most account takeovers start with a stolen password. Two-step sign-in stops the large majority of them.
  3. Do people use a password manager instead of shared or reused passwords? A shared spreadsheet of passwords is one leak away from a breach.
  4. When someone leaves, is their access removed the same day? Former staff with working accounts are a common and silent risk.
  5. Are computers and phones updated automatically? Out-of-date devices are the easiest way in for attackers.
  6. When IT breaks, do you know who to call and how fast they’ll respond? Without a clear owner, small problems quietly eat hours of your team’s time.
  7. Can you see your key numbers without building a spreadsheet by hand? Manual reports are slow, easy to get wrong and usually out of date.
  8. Is repetitive work, like copying data between apps, automated? Ten minutes of copy and paste a day adds up to a full week every year.
