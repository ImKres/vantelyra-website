Cookie Policy

This page explains how cookies and similar technologies may relate to the Vantelyra Digital Solutions website.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that a website may place on a browser or device. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and identifiers used to support functionality, remember settings, measure activity, or deliver content.

2. Current Use on This Website

Vantelyra does not currently use analytics, advertising, personalization, or marketing cookies on this website. The site does not use cookies to build visitor profiles or track visitors across websites.

Your selected light or dark theme is saved in your browser using local storage under the key vantelyra-theme. If you haven’t chosen a theme, the site follows your device’s light or dark setting. This preference stays on your device and can be cleared through your browser settings.

The hosting and security infrastructure, including Cloudflare Turnstile when the protected inquiry form is enabled, may process limited technical information or use strictly necessary technologies to deliver pages, verify legitimate form submissions, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability. These technologies are not used by Vantelyra for advertising.

3. Third-Party Websites

If you open an email application or follow a link to a social platform or another website, that third party may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Its privacy and cookie notices apply to activity on that service.

4. Your Browser Choices

Most browsers allow you to review, block, or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary technologies may affect how some websites function. Because this website currently has no non-essential cookie categories, Vantelyra does not display a cookie-consent banner.

5. Future Changes

If Vantelyra later adds analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other services that use non-essential cookies, this policy will be updated and an appropriate consent control will be added before those technologies are activated where required.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies or this policy, email hello@vantelyra.com.
