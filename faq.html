Questions Owners Ask Us

Straight answers to what small business owners ask us most. Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How much does it cost?

The first consultation and a first look at your setup are free. After that, every project gets a fixed quote before work starts, so you always know the price in advance. Monthly support plans are priced per user.

Do we need a long-term contract?

No. Projects are scoped and priced one at a time, and monthly support runs month to month.

You work remotely. How does support work?

We use secure remote-access tools, screen sharing and the chat or email your team already uses. Almost everything a small business needs, from accounts and security to cloud apps and data, can be managed remotely.

Which time zones do you cover?

We’re based in the Philippines (UTC+8) and agree support hours that overlap with your working day before any plan starts.

Who will actually do the work?

The specialists in our team. Each request has a named owner, so you always know who to talk to.

How do you handle our passwords and data?

We never ask for passwords by email or chat. We use our own accounts with two-step sign-in, keep credentials in a password manager, give ourselves only the access the work needs, and remove it when the work ends. We’re happy to sign an NDA.

What tools do you work with?

Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Power BI, Excel, Power Automate, Zapier, Make and WordPress, plus the common cloud apps small businesses run on. If you use something else, ask us.

Not sure where to start? Start with a conversation.

Tell us what’s slowing your team down. We’ll look into your setup and tell you plainly what we’d fix first, with no cost and no obligation.
