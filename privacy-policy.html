Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Vantelyra Digital Solutions may receive through this website, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, selected service, project details, and any other information included in your message.

The website offers a protected inquiry form and a separate Email us link. When direct form submission is enabled, the information you enter is securely transmitted for security verification and email delivery. The Email us link instead opens your device’s configured email application, where you review and send the message yourself.

Our hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, security tokens, and request logs, to operate the website, verify legitimate submissions, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Review and respond to inquiries.
  • Understand your project, prepare recommendations, or discuss a proposal.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the website and our communications.
  • Maintain appropriate business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Data Storage and Security

Messages you send may be stored in the email systems used by you and Vantelyra. We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Third-Party Services

The IT health check on our home page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us or stored, unless you choose to include them in an inquiry.

We may rely on service providers for website hosting, email, security, and related business operations. The protected inquiry flow uses Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse prevention and the existing GoDaddy Professional Email service for message delivery. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services or meet legal requirements. If you follow a link to a social platform or another third-party website, that service’s own terms and privacy practices apply.

5. Data Retention

We retain inquiry and business correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active engagement, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the communication and relationship.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to be informed, access your personal information, request correction, object to certain processing, request deletion or blocking, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, request data portability, and lodge a complaint with the appropriate data protection authority.

To exercise a request, contact us and describe the information or communication involved. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

7. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your personal information, email hello@vantelyra.com.
